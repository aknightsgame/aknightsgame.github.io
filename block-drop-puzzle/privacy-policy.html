Privacy Policy

Block Drop Puzzle  ·  Last updated: 15 April 2026

Block Drop Puzzle ("the app") is developed by A Knight's Game. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The developer does not directly collect or store any personal information. The app saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your best score and coin balance
  • Achievement progress
  • Settings preferences (sound, music, haptics)
  • Whether you have purchased the Remove Ads upgrade

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data

On iOS, you will be asked for permission before any advertising identifier is accessed. You can change this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For more information on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time purchase to remove advertisements, processed through the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive or store any payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
